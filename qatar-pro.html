Qatar PRO & Government Services

Build an understanding of common administrative workflows connected with Qatar business services and PRO support.

Learning areas

What the programme can cover.

  • Overview of Qatar business administration and PRO responsibilities
  • Introduction to MOCI and commercial registration-related workflows
  • Awareness of Hukoomi and relevant e-service journeys
  • Introduction to Ministry of Labour service processes
  • MOI-related administrative service awareness
  • Baladiya and Qatar Chamber service context
  • Establishment records, document preparation and follow-up practices
  • Professional communication, checklists and file coordination

Learning approach

Programme content, duration, batch schedule and delivery format will be confirmed by the academy. Enquire to receive the latest course outline.

Training is educational and does not provide government-system access, authority to represent clients or official approval.
