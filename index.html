Civil Services — Structured Learning Programme

Explore a guided academic pathway with dedicated modules, practical learning resources and a clear progression from foundation to final preparation.

Sub-Page 1 of 8 • Prelims

UPSC Prelims Course (GS Paper I)

Concept clarity, elimination logic, and high-frequency revision for Polity, Economy, History, Geography, Environment, and Science & Tech.

Core Static Subjects

Indian Polity & Constitution, Modern/Ancient/Art & Culture, Geography, Economy, and Environment.

Accuracy & Negative Marking

Structured practice to manage 1/3rd negative marking and avoid careless elimination errors.

PYQ & Revision Cycles

Previous year question pattern mapping and multi-pass revision sheets.

Sub-Page 2 of 8 • Mains

UPSC Mains Course (GS I–IV, Essay & Optional)

Analytical depth, multi-dimensional structuring, governance linkage, and balanced administrative conclusions.

GS Papers I, II & III

Society, Governance, Constitution, International Relations, Economic Development, Security & Environment.

GS Paper IV (Ethics)

Ethical frameworks, integrity, public service values, and real-world administrative case studies.

Essay & Optional Guidance

Philosophical and socio-economic essay structure plus optional subject alignment.

Sub-Page 3 of 8 • Personality Test (275 Marks)

UPSC Personality Test (Interview) Preparation

Focused on mental alertness, balanced judgment, honesty, clarity of thought, and administrative suitability.

Board Readiness

Calm demeanor, concise answers, and honest admission when a fact is unknown.

Balanced Standpoints

Presenting fair, constitutional, and citizen-centric perspectives without extreme bias.

One-on-One Feedback

Individual review of tone, structure, body language, and follow-up handling.

Sub-Page 4 of 8 • Interview Question Bank

What Questions Come in the UPSC Interview?

1. Background, Education & Work Experience Questions

"Why do you want to join Civil Services after your degree/work experience?" "How does your past work help you as a public administrator?"

2. Home State, District & Local Issues

"What are the top two developmental or administrative challenges in your home district/state, and how would you approach them?"

3. Governance, Ethics & Situational Dilemmas

"If local pressure conflicts with rule compliance or public interest, how do you handle it?" "How do you ensure transparency and empathy?"

4. Polity, Economy, Current Affairs & Follow-Ups

Questions probing constitutional principles, welfare delivery, global developments, and follow-up questions based on your own answers.

Sub-Page 5 of 8 • Mock Tests

UPSC Prelims, Mains & Interview Mock Tests We Conduct

Prelims Mock Tests

Timed sectional & full-length GS I and CSAT tests followed by error-log analysis (concept gap vs guess error).

Mains Mock Tests

Timed descriptive tests with structured evaluation on intro, sub-headings, examples, and way forward.

Interview Mock Panels

25–30 minute DAF-based mock board sessions with post-mock improvement notes.

Sub-Page 6 of 8 • CSAT & Answer Writing

CSAT (33% Qualifying) & Mains Answer Writing Drills

CSAT Practice

Reading comprehension accuracy, basic numeracy, and logical reasoning under strict time limits.

Daily Answer Writing

Word-limit discipline (150/250 words), crisp introductions, diagrams/points, and balanced conclusions.

Sub-Page 7 of 8 • DAF & Current Affairs

DAF Mapping & Current Affairs Linkage

DAF Deep Dive

Mapping every hobby, optional subject, job role, and hometown detail to potential board questions.

Current Affairs Integration

Connecting news events to constitutional provisions, policies, economy, and governance themes.

Sub-Page 8 of 8 • Study Roadmap

Integrated UPSC Preparation & Revision Roadmap

Phase 1: Foundation

Core static subjects, NCERT/standard concepts, and note-making habits.

Phase 2: Mocks & Writing

Regular Prelims mocks, CSAT drills, and Mains answer writing.

Phase 3: Interview & Final Polish

DAF preparation, mock boards, and high-yield revision.
