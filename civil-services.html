Programme overview

What each learning area covers

01 · PRELIMS

Prelims Course

Build conceptual clarity across key General Studies areas and develop a consistent approach to objective-question practice and revision.

Indian PolityHistoryGeographyEconomyEnvironmentScience & Technology
02 · MAINS

Mains Course

Develop structured understanding of General Studies themes, organise notes and practise presenting clear, relevant written responses.

03 · INTERVIEW

Interview Preparation

Prepare for formal discussion through communication practice, reflective exercises and guidance on presenting your perspective clearly.

04 · PRACTICE

Interview Questions

Explore practice questions around education, interests, current affairs, public issues and situational judgement.

05 · ASSESSMENT

Mock Tests

Use practice assessments to become familiar with question formats, time management and post-test review.

06 · SKILLS

CSAT & Answer Writing

Work on comprehension, logical reasoning, basic numeracy and the organisation of concise written answers.

07 · AWARENESS

DAF & Current Affairs

Build awareness of important developments and prepare to discuss your background, interests and experiences in a considered way.

08 · PLANNING

Study Roadmap

Organise subject coverage, practice sessions and revision into a manageable personal study plan.
